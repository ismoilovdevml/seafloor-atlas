## Ocean volume and mean depth (GEBCO_2026 15″)

* Ocean area 361.4 million km², mean depth **3,702 m**, volume **1.338 billion km³**.
* Charette & Smith (2010): 3,682 m and 1.332 billion km³. Hotham (2026, 10 km grid): 3,675 m and 1.33 billion km³.
* Ocean = TID ≠ 0 and below sea level, Caspian excluded. Water under floating ice shelves is not counted (the grid holds the ice surface there), which makes our area and volume slightly smaller.
