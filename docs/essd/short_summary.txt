We present a dataset that records, for every 15 arc-second cell of the ocean, in which of the eight GEBCO bathymetry releases from 2019 to 2026 the seafloor depth is coded as directly measured. The measured share grows from 12.4 % to 30.8 %, but in every new release 0.8 to 4.6 million square kilometres lose the measured code, and the share depends on the cell size used. The dataset makes these figures reproducible from public files.
