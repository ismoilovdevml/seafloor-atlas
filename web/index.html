      • Depth certainty

      • A second global model (AUST_2026_BAT) minus GEBCO 2026, shown only deeper than 1 km and where most of the cell is unmeasured. Both lean on satellite gravity, so agreement does not prove a depth right: read it as a lower bound on the uncertainty.

        Yuan & Yang (2026), doi:10.5281/zenodo.22711109, CC BY 4.0.

      • How sure a machine-learning model using SWOT satellite gravity is of its own predicted depth, in three classes. It describes that model, not the GEBCO grid; cells with a ship sounding are left clear.

        Nilsson, Phrampus, Sandwell et al. (2025), doi:10.5281/zenodo.17673974, CC BY 4.0.

      • Southern Ocean seals carrying depth tags, compared with the chart.

        Coloured dots: places where seals dived more than 20 m deeper than GEBCO 2026 allows, beyond each dive’s position error. Faint dots: where seals dived at all. A dive can show the chart is too shallow, never too deep. Click a dot for its numbers.

        SO-ABBA v1.0 (Hindell, McMahon, Jonsen et al. 2026), doi:10.5281/zenodo.19121676, CC BY 4.0.

      • What to show

        A group shows where its OBIS records are, per 1.4° cell.

        Records show where people looked as much as where life is: coasts, rich countries and research cruises dominate. ES50 corrects for the number of records, not for where or what was sampled. AquaMaps is a model, smooth by design. Click the map for a cell’s most-recorded species.

        OBIS (2026) www.obis.org; OBIS speciesgrids (OBIS + GBIF), CC BY-NC 4.0; AquaMaps (version 10/2019), CC BY-NC 3.0.
