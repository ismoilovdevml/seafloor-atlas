East Pacific Rise

The fastest-spreading major ridge: near 30° S the Pacific and Nazca plates part at about 15 cm a year, so the rise is broad and smooth instead of a deep rift valley.

Explore East Pacific Rise in 3D

Where it is

East Pacific Rise is centred near 20.00° S, 113.67° W. In the 3D globe you can turn on What's actually measured to see which parts of this seafloor were surveyed by ships and which are only estimated from satellite gravity, draw a depth profile across it, or change the sea level.

About the data

Depths come from the GEBCO 2026 global grid (15 arc-seconds, about 460 m at the equator). Across the whole ocean, 30.8 % of the seafloor is directly measured; the rest is estimated. Read how this is computed.
