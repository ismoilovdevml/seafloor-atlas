How much of the seafloor in Joint regime area: United States / Russia is mapped?

50.6 % of the seafloor in the Joint regime area: United States / Russia is directly measured in the GEBCO 2026 grid, mostly by ship sonar. It is too small (under 10,000 km²) to be ranked reliably.

What these numbers mean

"Directly measured" means the cell is coded TID 10–17 (multibeam, single beam, lidar, seismic or other direct soundings) in the GEBCO 2026 Type Identifier grid; everything else is estimated, mostly from satellite gravity. The numbers describe what has been shared with GEBCO: national and commercial surveys that were not contributed count as unmeasured. Zone boundaries come from Marine Regions (World EEZ v12, CC BY 4.0), rasterised on a 1′ grid, so very small zones carry errors of a few percent.
